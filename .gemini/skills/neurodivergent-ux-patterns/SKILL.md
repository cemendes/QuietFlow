# Neurodivergent Cognitive UX Patterns

Use this skill when designing, reviewing, or implementing features across QuietFlow and neurodivergent productivity applications.

## 1. The 5 Cognitive Pillars
1. **Task Initiation & "Wall of Awful"**: Deconstruct monolithic tasks into 3–5 bite-sized micro-steps (<5 min starter actions) using AI or heuristics ("Magic Slicer").
2. **Time Blindness & Sensory Pacing**: Replace stressful ticking countdown clocks with silent, visual perimeter sweeps (e.g. 25-minute glowing circular aura in "Zen Theater").
3. **Working Memory Leakage**: Offer a guilt-free "Breadcrumb Trail" re-entry banner when returning after >15m idle time.
4. **Triage & Decision Fatigue**: Never present more than 3–5 items at once; use binary NOW vs. LATER focus buckets.
5. **Dopamine & Micro-Rewards**: Pair task completion with instant tactile auditory feedback (Web Audio mechanical clicks) and visual celebrations (30+ varied kid-friendly cartoon, unicorn, and meme particle bursts).

## 2. Visual Palette Guidelines
- **Canvas**: Warm Sand (`#FAF9F6` / Linen) to eliminate screen glare and reduce sensory fatigue.
- **Accent**: Forest Emerald (`#065F46`) for grounded, calming focus.
- **Priority**: Muted Terracotta (`#C25E3E`) and Amber (`#D97706`).

## 3. Data Integrity & Frictionless Capture
- **Local Markdown**: Single source of truth with zero telemetry.
- **Auto-Seeding**: Creating a folder immediately seeds its primary note (`Folder/Folder.md`) and defaults note naming to `Folder - Month DD.md`.
